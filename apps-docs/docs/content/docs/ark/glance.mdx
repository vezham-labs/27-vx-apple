---
title: Glance
---

Option-click a link to preview it over the current page. Press Esc or click outside the card to close it. Press ⌘Return or choose **Open as a Tab** to keep the preview. A finished page stays loaded when the tab uses the same website profile and engine and the URL needs no cleanup. Links inside the card open there. ⌘-click and new windows open as tabs. Previews from Private use the private website store.

Code: `NavigationRequest.wantsGlance` (set by WebKitPage for Option-click on http(s) links, before
Safari's option-click download), `TabController.page(_:decide:)`, `Mac/Sources/Views/Glance.swift`.

Hover previews, previews from the sidebar or ⌘K results, and a configurable modifier remain open.
